Our three pillars

Stronger communities. Brighter futures.

We work alongside community leaders to identify the families facing the greatest hardship, then bring help that lasts.

Education

School supplies and learning programs for children and their parents, so whole families can learn and grow.

Public Health

Hygiene kits, toiletries and basic health support for families in need.

Support for the Elderly

Care for the aging neighbors in the communities we serve, who are too often forgotten.

Our story

A mission with roots in the Dominican Republic.

Our founder, Elsa Christensen, is from the Dominican Republic. She has watched children walk to school barefoot, carrying their books in plastic grocery bags because they have no backpack. She has seen students writing on scraps of paper with broken pencils.

For these children, education is their way out of poverty. We put the tools in their hands.

From Utah, Elsa built a team of volunteers who return to Independencia province every year, working hand in hand with local leaders to reach the children who need it most.
